About GramConnect

GramConnect is a digital platform for rural services and livelihoods. It helps villagers find government welfare schemes, get guidance on crop markets, connect with healthcare, and discover local jobs.

The interface is simple, accessible and low-bandwidth, so it works on basic phones and slow networks, with language support for every region.

Our Mission

We want every village to have the same access to information and opportunity that cities enjoy. Too many families miss out on schemes they qualify for, sell crops below fair prices, or travel far for basic healthcare, simply because the right information never reached them.

GramConnect closes that gap. Volunteers and local administrators keep the information accurate and up to date, and an audio overview helps people who are more comfortable listening than reading.

Who It Is For

Farmers, village families, job seekers, health workers and panchayat volunteers. Anyone in a rural community who needs trusted information, in their own language, without needing a fast connection or an expensive phone.
